06 HANDLING ON-THE-SPOT FOLLOW-UP

How to deal with an
unexpected follow up

The first answer is often where the real conversation begins.

But, your answer can be questioned further.

“ Why did you say that? ”
“ How can you justify that? ”
“ But what about...? ”
BOARD MEMBER 2:

“You suggested increasing agricultural subsidies to curb rural distress. Wouldn't that inflate the fiscal deficit and crowd out capital expenditure?”

CANDIDATE:

“Sir, rather than universal price subsidies, direct targeted income transfers combined with decentralized cold chain infra would protect capital outlay...”

BOARD MEMBER 2 (INSTANT FOLLOW-UP):

“How can you justify that when power subsidies in your home state currently account for over 14% of the state budget?”

Practice handling the question after the question.
